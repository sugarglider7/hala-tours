Mood 1 of 7

Sand & engines

South of Agadir the tracks run through Berber villages and out onto dunes by the ocean at Tifnit. Bring sunglasses; you'll get sand everywhere.

Quads, a sandboard and a camel in one afternoon? Ask us — we combine.

  • A few hours or less

    Quad biking

    South of the city, through a Berber village and onto the dunes by Tifnit beach. About 90 minutes of riding, briefing first.

  • Half day

    Buggy ride

    Two-seat buggies through forest and dunes with a mint-tea stop. Morning or afternoon; good with kids beside you.

  • Half day

    Sandboarding

    Boards and a big dune. Best paired with the quads — ask us.

  • All day

    The Little Desert by 4x4

    Dunes and a short camel ride, the Souss-Massa coast, lunch in a Berber family's home, then the old medina of Tiznit. Out 08:30, back around 17:30.

Mood 2 of 7

Salt water

Agadir lives on its bay. Get on it, get in it, or follow the coast south for a long day by the sea.

  • A few hours or less

    Jet ski

    Twenty minutes, half an hour or a full hour on Agadir bay.

  • Half day

    Boat trip: fish, swim, lunch

    Out from the marina with fishing gear on board, a swim stop, then fish grilled on deck with Moroccan salad. Back at your hotel early afternoon.

  • Half day

    Surf lessons

    Beginner and intermediate lessons, with pickup from your hotel in Agadir. Kids from about seven — ask us.

  • All day

    Legzira & Aglou, with lunch

    Aglou beach, free time on Legzira's red-cliff beach, a fish lunch by the ocean, home via Tiznit and the dunes. Out 08:00, back around 18:00.

Mood 3 of 7

Hooves, humps & crocodiles

Camels and horses out to the Souss river, where flamingos often feed in the shallows. And for the kids, a park full of Nile crocodiles.

Ask for the sunset ride.

  • A few hours or less

    Camel ride by the Souss river

    From the ranch out to the Souss river — flamingos, if they're about — past eucalyptus and a glimpse of the royal palace.

  • A few hours or less

    Camel ride & barbecue dinner

    Two hours on a camel to the river mouth, then dinner: barbecue, tagine or vegetarian.

  • A few hours or less

    Horse riding

    Along the Souss river and through the eucalyptus woods, with a guide — flamingos, if they're about.

  • Half day

    Crocoparc

    Nile crocodiles and big gardens about half an hour from town. We collect you from your hotel.

Mood 4 of 7

Medinas & markets

Ramparts, souks and big old squares: Marrakech and Essaouira for the day, or Agadir's own port and Souk El Had in a morning.

Marrakech is a long one: out at 7, home around 9.

  • All day

    Marrakech for the day

    Bahia Palace, the Koutoubia gardens, the souks and Jemaa el-Fna. Out 07:00, home around 21:00. Lunch isn't included.

  • All day

    Essaouira for the day

    Goats in the argan trees on the way, then the port, the Skala ramparts and the medina with a guide, and free time. Lunch is up to you.

  • Half day

    Agadir city tour

    Three hours: the Marina, the fishing port, the view from Agadir Oufella, an argan cooperative and an hour in Souk El Had.

Mood 5 of 7

Palms, gorges & mountain villages

Inland the air changes. Palm-lined gorges up the Imouzzer road, the walls of Taroudant, granite villages around Tafraout.

In summer we go to Paradise Valley in the morning. The afternoons are scorching.

  • Half dayAll day

    Paradise Valley

    Up the Imouzzer road to a palm-lined gorge between argan, almond and olive trees, with free time in Imouzzer village.

  • All day

    Taroudant & Tiout

    Taroudant's ramparts and souks, then the palm grove and kasbah at Tiout. Home by another road.

  • All day

    Tafraout & Tiznit On request

    Granite country: the Ameln valley, the painted rocks near Tafraout, then Tiznit's silver medina.

Mood 6 of 7

Go slow

Not every day needs an engine. Steam and argan oil, a long dinner with music — and, on request, a balloon at sunrise.

  • A few hours or less

    Hammam & massage

    A traditional hammam, then an hour's massage with argan oil. We drive you there and back.

  • A few hours or less

    Berber evening & dinner

    Dinner under caidal tents — pastilla, couscous, mint tea — with musicians, dancers and fantasia riders.

  • Hot-air balloon On request

    A sunrise flight, mint tea before and breakfast after. Ask us whether it can fly on your dates.

Mood 7 of 7

Two days, one night away

Sleep in the desert at Zagora or El Borj, or do Marrakech and Essaouira together. All three are on request — send us your dates and we'll check whether one can run.

  • Two days

    Zagora desert, 2 days On request

    Over to the Draa valley via Taroudant and Taliouine, a camel to camp, a night in the desert, sunrise on the dunes.

  • Two days

    El Borj desert, 2 days On request

    Tiznit and Legzira on the way, sunset in the El Borj dunes and a night in tents; camels and the Tighmert palm grove next day.

  • Two days

    Marrakech & Essaouira, 2 days On request

    Marrakech's palaces and souks, a night in a hotel, then Essaouira's medina and the coast road home.
