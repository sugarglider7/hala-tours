Two days, one night away

Marrakech & Essaouira, 2 daysOn request

Two days

Two cities without rushing either. Marrakech's palaces, souks and Jemaa el-Fna at night, a night in a hotel — then over to Essaouira's medina by the sea and home along the coast road. On request.

At a glance

Timing
2 days, 1 night in a hotel — back in Agadir around 18:00 on day 2

On request

This one doesn't run every week. Message us with your dates and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

What happens

  1. Day 1: Marrakech — the palaces and the souks.
  2. Jemaa el-Fna in the evening, and a night in a hotel.
  3. Day 2: across to Essaouira and its medina.
  4. Lunch by the sea.
  5. Home to Agadir along the coast road, around 18:00.

Good to know

  • Walking shoes for two city days.
  • A jacket for windy Essaouira.
  • Message us with your dates; we'll confirm the hotel and what's included.
