Hooves, humps & crocodiles

Camel ride & barbecue dinner

A few hours or less

Two hours on a camel through the countryside to the mouth of the Souss river — flamingos, if you're lucky — then dinner at the base: barbecue, a tagine, or vegetarian if you prefer.

Ask about this day on WhatsApp

At a glance

Timing
About 2 hours on the camel, then dinner
Food
Dinner: barbecue, tagine or vegetarian

Times are usual times — we confirm yours when you book.

What happens

  1. Meet your camel at the base, where the horses live too.
  2. About two hours out through the countryside.
  3. The mouth of the Souss river — flamingos, if they're about.
  4. Back for dinner: barbecue, tagine or vegetarian.

Good to know

  • Tell us about any food needs when you book.
  • Long trousers and a layer for after sunset.
  • Hungry kids? Dinner comes after the ride — bring a snack.

Go late in the afternoon and you ride back into the sunset.
