Finding us

  1. We're inside the Hotel Hamilton on Boulevard Mohammed V, in the centre of Agadir.
  2. Come in through the hotel's main entrance. Can't see us straight away? Ask at reception.
  3. Staying at another hotel? That's fine — on almost every trip we collect you from your hotel in Agadir.

Getting here

From the airport
Agadir–Al Massira airport is about 23 km away — around 30 minutes by road. We can collect you.
By bus
There's a city bus stop about 200 m from the hotel.
By taxi
Ask for the Hotel Hamilton on Boulevard Mohammed V.

Landing or leaving? We do airport transfers too.

Ayoub and the team
