# Provenance of halatoursagadir.com text & photos (checked 2026-10-02)

## Verdict
The current site is a **Maroc Annuaire (marocannuaire.org) multi-tenant agency template**. Hala's product **text and most product photos are not Hala's own**: they are copied from other operators' listings or shared with other agencies on the same platform. Hala's **own** imagery is the WhatsApp gallery (Nov 2022), the 8 "By owner" photos on its Google listing, and two product photos that also appear in that gallery (quad, jet ski).

## Evidence
- Template: footer "Réalisé par : Maroc Annuaire"; same `de_sv.php?n=…&i=…&p=<tenant>&c=<cat>` / `p.php?c=` / `img_sv/produit/<tenant>-…` URL scheme as excursionagadiractivity.com (`p=excursionagadir`), agadiratlanticexcursion.com, agadirloisirsquad.com (`agadirexperience-…` images), agadirbesttours.com (offline). Hala's tenant slug is **`agadirridertours`** (in every product URL, image name and hidden form field `page`) — the account was apparently created/cloned under another name. No live "Agadir Rider Tours" site or business was found (agadirridertours.com does not resolve; web search finds nothing).
- Leftovers from other tenants/templates: a commented-out booking button pointing to `https://www.agadirbesttours.com/contact.php` (another agency, Cité Dakhla, +212 665 196 923); education-template junk (counters "7096 ÉTUDIANTS ACTIFS / 508 COURS EN LIGNE / 167 ANNÉE D'HISTOIRE", "S'inscrire" buttons, pre-registration form with "Formation" + date of birth, empty categories bachelor / masters-europeen / le-mba-europeen / licence-professionnelle / master-specialise).
- **Photos shared with other agencies (perceptual hash distance 0–7, i.e. same picture):** quad `agadir.quad.biking2` = agadiratlanticexcursion quad2; horse `horse.riding.agadir3` = excursionagadir cheval.balade; camel `balade.chameau` ≈ excursionagadir camel; boat `boat.trip` & `excursion.bateau` & `excursion.bateau3` = agadiratlanticexcursion "ballade en mer"; buggy `buggy3` = excursionagadir buggy3; Marrakech `marrakech` = agadirexperience marrakech; `marrakech4` = agadiratlanticexcursion; Crocoparc `croco.parc3/4`, `crocoparc` = agadiratlanticexcursion / excursionagadir; Taroudant `excursion.tout.taroudant` = excursionagadir taroudant. The rest of the product photos are visibly generic web/stock imagery (Koutoubia, Majorelle, Merzouga camel caravans, Cappadocia-style balloon fields, studio hammam, sandboarding action shots, Legzira arch postcard, Tafraout painted rocks) — no Hala branding, staff or vehicles.
- **Text copied from other operators:** Sandboarding page = Tripadvisor product "Sandboarding guided experience from Agadir" (d26351885) word for word; Petit Désert page = holidaystaghazout.com "Excursion The Little Sahara (Tiznit – Tifnit – Massa)" ("Departure at 08:30 south direction, after a good hour drive (40km)… troglodyte houses, blue boats"); quad page sentence "quad biking and buggy tour are organized at the same place…" = GetYourGuide quad listing t151535; Marrakech FR intro shared with agadirloisirsquad; Legzira FR "chanteur russe" sentence shared with agadiratlanticexcursion. Text overlap with the three sibling sites is otherwise low, so pages are a patchwork from various sources.
- Stale itinerary facts inside the copied text: Tifnit's fishing/troglodyte village was demolished at the end of 2023 (lejardinauxetoiles.net); Legzira copy speaks of "huge arches" (the big arch collapsed in 2016 — general knowledge, not re-verified here); Crocoparc opening hours are a third party's.

## Consequences for the new site
- Never reuse product copy verbatim; rewrite from facts only. Never present shared/stock product photos as Hala's trips, vehicles, boats, horses or guides.
- Place-only stock (Marrakech, Essaouira, Paradise Valley, Taroudant, Tafraout, Legzira) may be CONTEXT-ONLY at most, and better replaced by clearly non-deceptive place imagery chosen by the design phase.
- First-party candidates: see ASSET_INVENTORY.md (gallery + Google owner photos).
